# Measurement and scoring rules

- Search Console supplies average search position, clicks and impressions for authorized properties. It does not supply live rankings, commercial search volumes, keyword difficulty, CPC, competitor keywords or intent labels. Those unsupported values are unavailable. CTR is total clicks / total impressions, and position is weighted by impressions. Zero impressions means no position. Previous-period comparisons use the equally long immediately preceding period and require actual data in both periods.
- Daily measurements are unique by project/query/page/date/device/country, preserving separate pages and complete query strings. Google's API returns top rows and omits anonymized queries; pagination cannot recover omitted rows. Display queries stop at 10,000 rows and warn when capped. Narrow the date range for complete displayed/exported subsets.
- Organic queries are discovered from GSC independently of tracked keywords. Opportunity candidates meet the configured minimum impressions, maximum CTR and position interval. Default: at least 100 impressions, CTR below 2%, average position 4–20. These thresholds are editable per project and do not forecast traffic gains.
- Backlinks come from uploaded files, not a nonexistent Google Links API. Source-domain-only exports remain domain-only records. Target-only and anchor-only exports are stored as aggregate snapshots in import history; no backlink relationships are inferred. CSV uploads accept up to 500 rows and 1 MB. Spreadsheet-format uploads are not implemented; export CSV from your spreadsheet first.
- Imported relationships are unverified observations. Unknown link type, anchor, target and authority remain unknown. First/last observation dates mean import dates, not proven discovery dates. Imported report dates are not independently verified. Reimport keys include source domain, source URL, target and anchor; reviews and first import date survive reimport.
- Risk is a Razza heuristic, not an official Google toxicity score. Points: weak TLD 10, low known PageRank 20, repeated commercial anchor 25, suspicious source URL 15, repeated source-domain pattern 10. Scores normalize against configured weights and cap TLD-only risk below 30. Commercial anchor evidence requires at least five repeated anchors and at least half of known anchors. Sitewide evidence is a repeated-domain heuristic, not proof of sitewide placement. Unknowns are retained in structured signals. Classification: 0–29 low, 30–59 moderate, 60–79 high, 80–100 critical. Project risk is the arithmetic mean of evaluated records, with record coverage and separate known-attribute coverage. No automated disavow or penalty prediction is performed.
- Audit percentage is the proportion of pages in the latest completed crawl without critical findings, not a Google SEO score. The parser checks actual server HTML. JavaScript-rendered content, resource sizes beyond the fetched HTML, external link reachability, structured-data semantic validity, sitemap indexes and browser rendering are not fully audited. Findings describe only observed evidence. Broken internal links are identified for targets actually crawled. Robots disallow or unsupported/private DNS causes a visible failed job, not a clean audit.
- Duplicate title/description checks use pages in a completed run. Audit-generated issues reopen when redetected and resolve only after a completed crawl successfully checks their URL and no longer reports their finding. Cancelled/failed runs do not resolve issues. Manual tasks are not automatically resolved.
- PageSpeed category scores are Lighthouse laboratory results. LCP, CLS and INP fields come only from returned URL-level CrUX data; absent field data remains unavailable. Lighthouse values are retained separately. Google may omit field metrics or reject requests under quota.
- CSV exports contain actual currently filtered records, project/domain/source/range metadata, and formula injection protection. Email delivery is not configured; no sent/delivered status is fabricated. The report modal provides a local CSV download.
